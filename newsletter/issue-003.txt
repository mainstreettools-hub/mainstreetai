DO THIS FIRST
---------------------------------------
Set these five things before you share a booking link. Online booking only saves time if the calendar matches how you really work. Before you send anyone the link:

1. Block your real hours, including lunch and the time you need to drive between stops.
2. Set how long each service actually takes, not how long you wish it took.
3. Decide how far ahead people can book, and how late they can cancel.
4. Turn on reminders so no-shows get a nudge the day before.
5. Book a fake appointment yourself from your phone to see what a customer sees.

Then put the link on your website, your Google Business Profile and your voicemail greeting.
