TOOL OF THE WEEK: JOBBER (Field Service & Scheduling)
---------------------------------------
Quoting, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and getting paid, all in one app built for home-service crews. If you're still running jobs off a whiteboard and texts, this is the easiest place to start.

Price: Core $29/mo billed annually ($49 month-to-month), Connect $99 ($139), Grow $149 ($199) for one user. Extra users $29/mo. Card fees 2.9% + 30c. (Checked on Jobber's site, Oct 2026.)
Good: published pricing, easy for crews to learn, free trial.
Watch for: add-ons like the AI receptionist cost extra. Setup takes about 1-3 days.

TRY THIS BEFORE YOU BUY
---------------------------------------
Test an AI receptionist in 10 minutes. During the free trial, call it yourself from a different phone and play three real customers:

1. Someone with an emergency ("water's coming through my ceiling"). Does it flag it as urgent or transfer you?
2. Someone asking a price you don't quote over the phone. Does it make something up?
3. Someone who wants Tuesday at 2. Does the booking actually land on your calendar?

Then read the transcript or message it sent you. If you'd be embarrassed for a customer to hear any of it, keep looking.
