Tool of the week
ServiceAgent
AI Phone Receptionist

An AI front office built for home-service shops. It answers calls, books jobs, sends invoices and runs your customer follow-ups, all from one account that runs on credits. Good for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing shops that can't get to the phone while they're on a job.

Price: Launch plan is free (pay as you go for AI). Core $49/mo for 2,000 credits (about $39/mo billed yearly). Growth $119/mo, Franchise $349/mo. AI voice uses 15 credits a minute, so Core covers about 133 minutes if every credit goes to calls. (Checked on ServiceAgent's site, Oct 2026.)

Good: free to start, made for the trades, and calls, booking and invoicing live in one place. Claiming a number takes minutes; full setup takes 1–2 days.

Watch for: texts and ads use the same credits as calls, so your voice minutes are a ceiling, not a promise. Payment and ad fees come on top of the plan.

Do this first
Call your own business tonight. It takes 10 minutes and tells you exactly what a new customer hears when you can't pick up. From a phone that isn't saved in your contacts:
  1. Call during a busy hour and let it ring out. Count the rings before anything answers.
  2. Listen to your voicemail greeting. Does it say your business name, and does it tell people when you'll call back?
  3. Leave a message, then time how long it takes before anyone returns it.
  4. Call again after hours and see if anything different happens.
  5. Text the same number and see if anyone answers.
If any of that made you wince, that's where an answering service or an AI receptionist earns its keep. Check if your website is leaking jobs
