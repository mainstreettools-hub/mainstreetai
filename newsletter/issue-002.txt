DO THIS FIRST
---------------------------------------
Write your answer sheet before you set up any AI. A phone agent or chat bot can only repeat what you give it. Spend 15 minutes on one page that covers:

1. Your hours and the towns or zip codes you serve.
2. What you charge for a service call or estimate, or the exact words to use if you don't quote over the phone.
3. What counts as an emergency, and who gets called when one comes in.
4. How someone books, and what you need from them (address, photos, best callback time).

Paste it into whatever tool you try. If a question isn't on the sheet, the AI shouldn't be guessing at it.
