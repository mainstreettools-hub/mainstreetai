## 4. Canceling
The Business can cancel any time, with no fee. Send notice by email to mainstreettools@gmail.com. Canceling takes effect at the end of the paid month. No refund for a partial month. If the setup was split, the second $149 payment is still owed.

## 5. Tool bills
The Business pays each tool vendor directly, on its own card. MainStreetAI never pays a vendor bill for the Business, never puts its own card on the Business's accounts, and never holds the Business's accounts.

## 6. Who owns what
The Business owns its accounts, logins, customer lists, data and phone number. MainStreetAI only has the access the Business gives it. When the agreement ends, MainStreetAI will hand over anything it set up and remove its own access within 7 days.

## 7. No promises on results
MainStreetAI will do the work with care, but does not promise any number of calls, jobs, reviews or sales. Tools are run by their vendors, and MainStreetAI is not responsible for a vendor's outages, price changes or mistakes.

## 8. The Business's part
The Business will:
- follow Google's review rules: ask all customers, offer no rewards for reviews, and never screen out unhappy customers;
- only text customers who agreed to get texts, and honor "STOP" requests;
- tell MainStreetAI when its hours, services or answers change.
