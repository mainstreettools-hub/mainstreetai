## Questions to ask

### Trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, handyman) – phone answerer
1. "When you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?"
2. "About how many calls a week go to voicemail? Do people leave messages, or just hang up?"
3. "What's an urgent call for you, and what can wait until you call back?"

Good fit if: they miss calls often, they're the main person answering, and they can name a job they lost to a slow callback.

### Trades – reviews (if they say calls are fine)
1. "How many Google reviews do you have right now?"
2. "Do you ask customers for reviews today? How?"
3. "Do you get customers' emails, or just phone numbers?"

### Auto repair
1. "Who answers the phone when the bays are full?"
2. "Do you ask for reviews when people pick up their car?"
3. "Do you collect customer emails on the work order?"

### Lawn care, cleaning, pet grooming
1. "How many customers do you finish with in a normal week?"
2. "How do new customers usually find you?"
3. "Would you or someone on your team add a customer to a list at the end of the day?"

Good fit if: steady weekly customers, low review count compared to how long they've been open, and someone who will add customers to the list.

### Salons and barbers
1. "Do people book online, by phone, or walk in?"
2. "How many Google reviews do you have? When did the last one come in?"
3. "Do you have client emails in your booking app?"

### Dental (reviews only)
1. "Does your office ask patients for Google reviews today?"
2. "Who would add patients to the list, and do you have their emails?"
3. "Is there anyone who has to approve patient emails, like an office manager?"

Never put health details in the sheet or emails. Name, email and visit date only.

## Before you say yes to a business, check
- They have a **verified Google Business Profile** (needed for reviews, and helpful for both).
- The owner can give 20 minutes on a weekday.
- For the phone option: you know the carrier, and the owner is OK changing call forwarding.
- The owner understands it's free for 2 weeks, then $299 + $49/mo (plus the tool bill for the phone option) if they keep it, and nothing if they don't.
