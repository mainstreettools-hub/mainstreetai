## Pick one

**Option A: A phone answerer for calls you can't get to**
When you're on a job, under a sink, or with a customer, calls still get answered. It picks up, answers simple questions (hours, services, area you cover), takes the caller's name, number and what they need, and sends you a summary by text or email right away. You keep your own phone number. Your calls just roll over to it when you don't answer.

**Option B: Automatic review requests**
After a job, your customer gets a short, friendly thank-you email with a link to leave a Google review. If they don't get to it, they get one gentle reminder a few days later. Then it stops. You can also text them the same link from your own phone with a saved message.

**Do I have to change my phone number?**
No. Your number stays the same. Unanswered calls forward to the answerer. That's a setting on your phone line that I'll help you change, and change back.

Yes, if we do it Google's way. We ask every customer, not just the happy ones. We never offer a discount or gift for a review. We never tell people what to write.
