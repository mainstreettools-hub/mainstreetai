# Pricing proposal: zero-traffic founding pricing (DRAFT, Stripe not changed)

**Why:** The site launched Oct 14 with no traffic history and 1 newsletter subscriber. $299/mo and $450/wk are hard to justify with no audience to show, and the first 7 pitches have had no replies. Logos and testimonials are worth more to us right now than small amounts of revenue.

| Offer | Now | Proposed | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding Listing | $49 Fast-Track | **Free for the first 10 vendors**, in exchange for logo use on a "Founding partners" strip and a one-line testimonial | Gives us social proof for later sales, and turns cold pitches into an easy yes |
| Fast-Track Listing | $49 | Keep $49 after the 10 free spots are gone | Low-friction paid entry |
| Featured Spot | $299/mo (FOUNDING50 = 50% off month 1) | **$49 for the first month for founding partners**, then $149/mo until we publish real traffic numbers, then $299 | Matches price to the audience we can actually show; lock in early buyers |
| Newsletter Banner | $450/wk | **Paused** until 500 subscribers; then about $50/wk, scaling with list size | Selling a banner to 1 reader isn't honest; resume once there's a real list |

**Rules:** No traffic or subscriber claims until Vercel Analytics has 30 days of data. Founding pricing expires on a set date (suggest Dec 31, 2026) or once the 10 spots are filled.
