# Other revenue ideas for MainStreetAI (zero-traffic stage)
Honest view: until search traffic shows up (weeks to months), every idea that depends on visitors will earn about $0. The ones that can earn early sell to **vendors** or monetize **one-to-one help**, not page views.

1. **Paid "setup help" referrals (done-for-you setup).** Partner with 1-3 freelancers/agencies who set up AI receptionists or Jobber/QuickBooks for trades. We send matched owners and take a 15-25% referral fee or a flat $50-$150. Dialzara, Smith.ai and Rosie already run agency/white-label programs, so setup partners exist. *Assessment:* best fit with what owners actually struggle with. Low volume early, but each lead is worth a lot. Needs a written agreement Ryan approves. Moderate effort.
2. **"Get matched" lead-gen form.** Owner answers 5 questions (trade, call volume, budget, current software). We send the top 2-3 picks by email (affiliate links where we have them), and with consent pass warm leads to vendors that pay per lead (Housecall Pro already pays per lead, and ServiceTitan pays $500 per demo). *Assessment:* highest long-term value per visitor. Needs a clear consent checkbox and a privacy note. Worthless until there's traffic, but cheap to build now.
3. **Sponsored head-to-head comparisons / "verified review" packages.** A vendor pays a flat fee ($99-$299 founding) for a hands-on test write-up and a clearly labeled sponsored comparison page, with our verdict left alone. *Assessment:* sellable before traffic because vendors want the backlink and the content. Credibility risk if not labeled clearly; must say "Sponsored" and keep the verdict independent. Low effort.
4. **Paid downloadable kits ($19-$49).** For example, an "AI receptionist script + setup pack for HVAC" or "switch to Jobber in a weekend" checklist, sold with Stripe Payment Links. *Assessment:* small but real money from newsletter signups. Grows the list as a lead magnet. Low effort with what we have. Expect a handful of sales a month at first.
5. **Association / franchise partnerships.** Offer trade associations a co-branded "AI tools guide for members" with member discounts negotiated from affiliate vendors (Ruby and Smith.ai already do association deals). *Assessment:* slow B2B sales cycle, but one deal brings a whole audience. Fits the trade-newsletter outreach list we already have. Outreach drafts go to Ryan first.

**Suggested order:** 3 (sell now with sponsor pitches) → 4 (cheap, uses the checklist funnel) → 2 (build the form now, monetize later) → 1 → 5.
