# Ad-library sponsor prospects (Meta / Google / public mirrors)

**Built:** Thu Oct 15, 2026 (ET)  
**Goal:** SaaS vendors that already pay for ads (AI receptionist, field service, scheduling, home-services CRM, business phone AI) — better sponsorship targets than cold lists with no budget signal.  
**Method:** Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center both blocked or returned empty SPA shells from this box (Meta HTTP 403; Google often 429 / JS-only). Counts and run status below come from public mirrors that read Meta’s library — mainly [Sprites](https://www.sprites.ai/software-ads) (read dates ~Oct 5–6, 2026) and [Crush](https://trycrush.ai/ad-library) for Smith.ai. Search URLs for live re-checks are listed per row.

**Live founding tiers** (see `category-sponsorship-proposal.md` / `/sponsor`): Trade Guide **$79/mo** · Category **$149/mo** · Category + Featured **$199/mo** · Featured Spot **$149/mo** (founding first-month offers may apply). Regular prices higher; founding through Dec 31, 2026 or until the slot fills. No traffic claims.

**Already-pitched cross-check:** `pitches.md`, `prospects.csv`, `oct18-asymmetry-followups.md`.

## Blockers

| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| Meta Ad Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`) | HTTP **403** from box curl |
| Google Ads Transparency (`adstransparency.google.com`) | **429** or large JS shell with no usable advertiser payload |
| Crush / Sprites | Usable public mirrors of Meta creatives and counts |

**Re-check search URLs (open in a browser):**
- Meta keyword: `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=US&q=COMPANY&search_type=keyword_unordered`
- Meta by page (after you find the Page): Ad Library → Advertisers → company name
- Google by domain: `https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=US&domain=example.com`
