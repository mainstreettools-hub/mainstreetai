## What the sponsor gets
- A labeled "Sponsored by ___" banner at the top of the category page (or trade guide), above the tool grid.
- Their tool card pinned first in that category's grid, marked "Sponsor".
- The same banner on the matching /solutions and /compare pages that list their category (e.g. a phone-receptionist sponsor shows on the receptionist solutions pages).
- One competitor-free month: we sell one sponsor per category at a time.
- Verdicts, pros/cons and rankings in the editorial sections don't change. Same rule as the Featured Spot.
